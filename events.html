Events

Columbus has no shortage of things to do throughout the year. The city hosts some seriously fun annual events, from massive sports festivals that draw athletes from all over the world to free summer concerts and fireworks on the riverfront. There's genuinely something going on every season, so no matter when you visit, you're probably landing right in the middle of something worth checking out!

The Ohio State Fair

Visit Website

A favorite family tradition since 1850, the Ohio State Fair is one of the longest-standing traditions in the state. It's packed with exhilarating Midway rides, world-class concerts, and the famous butter cow.

  • Dates: Late July through early August (annually)
  • Location: 717 E 17th Ave, Columbus, OH 43211
  • It has been held since 1850!

The Arnold Sports Festival

Visit Website

Founded by Arnold Schwarzenegger in 1989, this is the largest multi-sport festival in the world. It attracts over 20,000 athletes from 80 nations to compete in more than 60 sports, ranging from bodybuilding and martial arts to cheerleading and eSports.

  • Dates: Late February through early March
  • Location: 700 N High St, Columbus, OH 43215
  • It features more athletes than the Olympic Games!

Red, White & BOOM!

Visit Website

This is the largest Independence Day celebration in the Midwest, drawing over 400,000 visitors to Downtown Columbus. The event features a huge street festival, live music, a parade, and the most spectacular fireworks display in Ohio.

  • Dates: July 3rd every year
  • Location: Downtown Columbus & Scioto Mile, Columbus, OH 43215
  • General admission is free for everyone!

Columbus Arts Festival

Visit Website

Recognized as one of the top fine art festivals in the country, this event transforms the downtown riverfront into a massive outdoor gallery. It showcases over 200 juried artists from across the nation, alongside multiple stages for live music, dance, and theater.

  • Dates: Mid-June (Friday - Sunday)
  • Location: Scioto Mile Riverfront, Columbus, OH 43215
  • A Columbus cultural fixture since 1960!

Jazz & Rib Fest

Visit Website

This beloved summertime tradition offers three days of "hot ribs and cool jazz." Visitors can sample BBQ from award-winning pitmasters from across the country while enjoying continuous live performances from world-class jazz musicians on multiple stages.

  • Dates: Late July
  • Location: 233 S Civic Center Dr, Columbus, OH 43215
  • Admission is free, so you can save your money for the ribs!

Columbus Book Festival

Visit Website

The Columbus Book Festival is a massive, two-day celebration held at the historic Main Library and Topiary Park. It features over 200 national and local authors, panel discussions, and the popular "Indie Author Alley," where you can meet self-published creators.

  • Dates: Mid-July
  • Location: 96 S Grant Ave, Columbus, OH 43215
  • Admission is free and includes a huge "Friends of the Library" book sale!
