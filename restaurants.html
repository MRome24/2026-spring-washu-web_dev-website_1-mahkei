Schmidt's Sausage Haus

Visit Website

Located in the heart of historic German Village, Schmidt's has been a Columbus staple since 1886. It is famous for its authentic German buffet, award-winning sausages, and giant half-pound cream puffs.

North Market (Downtown)

Visit Website

The North Market is a vibrant public market that features over 30 independent merchants. It's the best spot to find a variety of international cuisines, fresh local produce, and unique gifts all under one roof.
