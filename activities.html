Activities

There are a lot of fun activities you can partake in throughout Columbus, OH!

Center of Science and Industry (COSI)

COSI is a science museum and research center located right in Downtown, Columbus (and next to the Scioto River, an amazing view!). It features more than 300 interactive exhibits which are within themed areas in the museum.

More Details:
  • Price: Varies between adults, youth, seniors, millitary, and teachers; Prices differ between purchasing tickets online or in-person - See website for more pricing details
  • Location: 333 W Broad St, Columbus, OH 43215
  • Hours of Operation: 10AM - 5PM

Scene75 Entertainment Center

Scene75 is a large indoor entertainment center that features arcade games, go-karts, laser tag, an indoor drop tower, and even an indoor roller coaster! There is plenty of food options and amazing prices that can be earned with tickets! Plus, it is located inside the Tuttle Mall, so you can do some shopping afterwards!

More Details:
  • See website for pricing details
  • Location: 5033 Tuttle Crossing Blvd, Columbus, OH 43016
  • Hours of Operation: Sundays - Thursdays: 12PM - 9PM | Fridays: 12PM - 11PM | Saturdays: 10AM - 11PM

Otherworld

Otherworld is a 32,000-square-foot immersive art installation that feels like stepping into a sci-fi movie. It features over 40 rooms filled with interactive tech, large-scale puzzles, and psychedelic secret passages. It’s essentially a "choose your own adventure" playground where the environment reacts to your movement.

More Details:
  • Price: Varies between adults, children, seniors and millitary; $2 fee applied to general admission - See website for timed tickets
  • Location: 5819 Chantry Dr, Columbus, OH 43232
  • Hours of Operation: Sundays - Thursdays: 10AM - 8PM | Fridays - Saturdays: 10AM - 10PM

Columbus Zoo and Aquarium

Consistently ranked as one of the best zoos in the country, this massive complex is home to more than 10,000 animals. In February, you can enjoy "Winter at the Zoo" with discounted admission and indoor exhibits like the Manatee Coast and the Discovery Reef Aquarium.

More Details:
  • Price: Varies between adults, children, and seniors; Discounted pricing available for those who live in Franklin County! - See website for details
  • Location: 4850 W Powell Rd, Powell, OH 43065
  • Hours of Operation: Daily: 10AM - 4PM (Winter Hours)

Franklin Park Conservatory and Botanical Gardens

This is a beautiful botanical landmark that features the iconic 1895 John F. Wolfe Palm House. It's famous for its collection of glass artwork by Dale Chihuly and its diverse "biomes" that let you walk from a Himalayan mountain range into a tropical rainforest in minutes.

More Details:
  • Price: Varies between adults, children, and seniors; Culture Fee is also charged on top of daily admission price -See website for tickets
  • Location: 1777 E Broad St, Columbus, OH 43203
  • Hours of Operation: Daily: 10AM - 5PM

Short North Arts District

Known as the "art and soul" of Columbus, the Short North is a vibrant neighborhood filled with local galleries, boutiques, and some of the best food in the city. It's perfect for a "Gallery Hop" or just walking around to see the murals and iconic lighted arches over High Street.

More Details:
  • Price: Free to explore
  • Location: N High St (Between Downtown and OSU)
  • Hours of Operation: Most shops/galleries: 11AM - 8PM (Varies by business)
